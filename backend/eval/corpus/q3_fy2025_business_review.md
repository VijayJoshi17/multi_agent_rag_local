# Q3 FY2025 Business Review (July–September 2025)

## Headline numbers

Revenue for the quarter was €48.2 million, up 23% year over year. Recurring fleet-subscription revenue was €31.5 million, or 65% of total revenue, compared with 58% a year ago. Gross margin improved to 61% from 57%, driven mainly by lower battery costs. Operating loss narrowed to €2.1 million.

We signed 19 new customers during the quarter and ended it with 4,860 robots under subscription. Net revenue retention was 128%.

## Regional performance

The DACH region (Germany, Austria and Switzerland) was again our largest market at 38% of revenue. The Nordics grew fastest, with revenue up 41% year over year, helped by two cold-storage deployments of the LR-400. North America represented 12% of revenue following the opening of our Chicago office in May.

## Products

The LR-400 accounted for 29% of new robot deployments, up from 18% in Q2. The Sparrow (LR-200) remains the volume product. Average deployment time from contract signature to go-live fell to 6 weeks.

## People

Headcount reached 412 employees at the end of September, up from 376 at the end of June. Engineering represents 46% of the company. Voluntary attrition over the trailing twelve months was 7%.

## Outlook

For Q4 FY2025 we expect revenue between €52 million and €54 million. Our priorities are completing the private-5G connectivity rollout and launching the Fleet API v3 beta.
