# New Hire Onboarding — Frequently Asked Questions

**What happens on my first day?**
Your first day starts at 09:30 at reception. You will pick up your laptop from the IT desk on the second floor, meet your onboarding buddy, and join the company welcome session at 14:00. Onboarding buddies are assigned by your manager before you start and stay with you for your first 90 days.

**When is my probation period?**
The probation period is three months for all permanent roles. You will have check-ins with your manager after 30, 60 and 90 days.

**How do I get access to the systems I need?**
Accounts for email, chat, 1Password and the HR system are created before your first day. Everything else — code repositories, the Fleet Console, design tools — is requested through the IT Helpdesk portal. Most requests are fulfilled within one working day.

**How do I connect to the internal network from home?**
Install the company WireGuard VPN profile from the self-service portal. You must be on the VPN to reach internal dashboards, but not for email or chat.

**When do I get paid?**
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the last working day before it.

**Who do I talk to about benefits?**
The People team handles benefits, leave and payroll questions. You can reach them in the #ask-people channel. For the details of leave and expenses, read the Employee Handbook.

**Is there a dress code?**
No. Visitors to customer sites must wear the safety shoes and high-visibility vest provided by the site.
