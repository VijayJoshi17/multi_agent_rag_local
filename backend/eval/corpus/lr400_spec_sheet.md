# LR-400 "Heron" — Heavy-Duty Autonomous Mobile Robot: Technical Specification

The LR-400 is Larkspur's heavy-duty autonomous mobile robot for pallet and cart transport in manufacturing plants and cold-chain warehouses. It shares the navigation stack of the LR-200 but is built for heavier loads and harsher environments.

## Physical

- Dimensions (L × W × H): 1,350 × 900 × 380 mm
- Weight (without payload): 310 kg
- Maximum payload: 1,200 kg
- Top speed: 1.5 m/s (limited to 0.8 m/s when carrying more than 800 kg)
- Turning: zero-radius turn on the spot
- Ingress protection: IP65

## Power

- Battery: 48 V lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4), 5.4 kWh, with a heated battery enclosure
- Runtime: up to 8 hours at a typical 60% duty cycle
- Charging: automatic docking; 0–80% in 90 minutes, full charge in 2 hours
- Battery service life: rated for 4,000 full charge cycles

## Sensors and safety

- Four safety lidars providing 360° coverage, PL d / SIL 2 certified
- Two 3D depth cameras and a load-presence sensor on the lift deck
- Emergency stop buttons on all four sides

## Connectivity

The LR-400 connects over Wi-Fi 6 or private 5G and is managed through the Larkspur Fleet API.

## Environment

Operating temperature is −25 °C to +40 °C, making the LR-400 suitable for cold-storage and freezer facilities.

## Warranty

Standard warranty is 36 months, extendable to 72 months with a Larkspur Care service contract.
