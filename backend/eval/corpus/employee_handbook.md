# Larkspur Robotics — Employee Handbook (2025 edition)

## 1. Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Outside those hours, teams organise their own schedules. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week; the remaining days are spent at one of our offices in Utrecht, Lyon or Kraków. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every twelve months.

Employees working from home receive a one-off home office allowance of €450 for furniture and equipment, plus a monthly internet stipend of €35. Equipment bought with the allowance stays with the employee.

## 2. Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to public holidays of the country they are employed in. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Vacation requests longer than ten consecutive working days must be submitted at least six weeks in advance.

Sick leave does not count against vacation. For absences longer than three consecutive days a doctor's note is required.

## 3. Parental and family leave

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first eighteen months after the child arrives. Employees caring for a seriously ill family member may take up to ten days of paid care leave per year.

## 4. Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of €1,800, usable for courses, conferences, certifications and books. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is booked through the travel policy below and is paid separately from the learning budget.

## 5. Travel and expenses

Business travel must be booked through the Navan travel portal. Train travel is required for any trip under five hours by rail. Economy class is the default for flights; premium economy is allowed for flights longer than seven hours.

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to €70 per day. Client entertainment requires pre-approval from a manager when it exceeds €250. Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense, with an itemised receipt, through the Pleo app. Claims filed later than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## 6. Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours (see the Information Security Policy).
